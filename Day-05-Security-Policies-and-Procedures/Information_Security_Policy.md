# SDS Information Security Policy — DRAFT

**Policy ID:** SDS-SEC-POL-001  
**Owner:** Information System Security Officer (ISSO)  
**Approver:** Executive Sponsor (pending)  
**Status:** Draft / Not approved  
**Review frequency:** Annually and after material change

## 1. Purpose and scope
Establish risk-based protection expectations for simulated SDS information systems, personnel, contractors, and data, including simulated Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) within the documented system boundary.

## 2. Governance
The executive sponsor owns enterprise risk decisions and approves policy exceptions. The ISSO coordinates risk assessments, control oversight, and reporting. System owners are accountable for security within their systems. Administrators implement approved technical controls; users follow policy and report concerns.

## 3. Core requirements
1. **Inventory and classification:** Maintain asset and data inventories, identify simulated CUI, and document authorized handling locations.
2. **Access:** Enforce least privilege, unique accounts, multifactor authentication where required, approved onboarding/change/offboarding, and periodic access review.
3. **Secure configuration:** Apply documented baselines, change control, vulnerability remediation, and protective logging.
4. **Information protection:** Use approved encryption, secure transfer, backup, and retention/disposal practices based on data classification and requirements.
5. **Incident response:** Report suspected incidents immediately through approved channels; preserve evidence and follow the incident response procedure.
6. **Third parties:** Evaluate service providers and cloud dependencies for security, CUI scope, and contract obligations before use.
7. **Monitoring:** Review security events, control performance, open risks, and corrective actions on a defined schedule.
8. **Training:** Require onboarding and recurring role-appropriate security awareness training.

## 4. Exceptions and enforcement
Exceptions require documented business justification, risk assessment, compensating safeguards, expiration date, and executive approval. Unapproved exceptions are prohibited. Violations are escalated through HR, legal, and security governance as appropriate.

## 5. Records and review
Maintain version history, approvals, exceptions, training acknowledgments, and evidence references. Review at least annually and when contracts, threats, systems, or regulations materially change.

## 6. References
NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5; NIST SP 800-171; CMMC Level 2; ISO/IEC 27001:2022. Applicability requires validation.

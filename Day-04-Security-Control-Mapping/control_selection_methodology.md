# Day 4 — Control Selection Methodology

## Objective
Trace identified risks to protective controls and create a reviewable, evidence-oriented control implementation plan.

## Approach
1. **Scope:** Start with SDS's simulated CUI system boundary and Day 2 asset inventory.
2. **Risk linkage:** Use Day 3 risk IDs, scenarios, likelihood, impact and treatment decision; reconcile the illustrative examples before publication.
3. **Applicability:** Identify applicable CMMC Level 2 / NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 practices; distinguish required practices from supplemental risk-based measures.
4. **Cross-reference:** Add related NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 and ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A controls as informative references, not one-to-one equivalents.
5. **Implementation:** Describe planned control operation, responsible owner, evidence, assessment method and status.
6. **Gap management:** Record unmet requirements, corrective action, milestones, accountable owner and expected residual risk.
7. **Governance:** Review with security leadership and system stakeholders. Acceptance of risk does not automatically waive contractual CMMC requirements.

## Implementation status definitions
- **Not assessed:** No evidence review completed.
- **Planned:** Control selected, not yet implemented.
- **Partially implemented:** Some requirements or assets covered, gaps remain.
- **Implemented (unverified):** Owner reports operation, evidence not yet tested.
- **Verified:** Assessment evidence supports effectiveness for the scoped environment.

## Validation notes
The mappings are educational starting points. Verify control text, applicability, and crosswalks against primary publications. No assessment results or compliance assertions are implied.

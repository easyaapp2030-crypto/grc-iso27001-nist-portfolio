# Evidence Collection and Validation Procedure

**Status:** Simulated draft | **Environment:** Sentinel Defense Solutions (fictional)

## 1. Purpose and scope
Establish a repeatable process for requesting, validating, and recording evidence that supports control implementation and assessment. Applies to the 12 illustrative controls in the Day 6 trackers.

## 2. Responsibilities
- **GRC Lead:** Defines scope, coordinates requests, records findings, and maintains traceability.
- **Control Owner:** Explains the safeguard and supplies approved evidence.
- **Assessor/Reviewer:** Independently checks evidence against the control objective.
- **Information Owner:** Authorizes access and determines sensitivity/retention requirements.

## 3. Collection workflow
1. Confirm system boundary, assessment period, and authoritative requirements.
2. Assign evidence request ID and control owner.
3. Specify requested artifact, time period, population, and acceptable formats.
4. Transfer via approved restricted repository; never place live logs, CUI, secrets, credentials, or sensitive configurations in public GitHub.
5. Check provenance, timestamp, completeness, integrity, and relevance to the scoped system.
6. Interview responsible staff and inspect/observe/test controls where appropriate; a document alone may not establish effectiveness.
7. Record result as **Not reviewed**, **Sufficient**, **Insufficient**, or **Needs follow-up**; separately record implementation state.
8. Log deficiencies for corrective action and assign an owner and due date.
9. Retain/dispose of artifacts according to approved policy and contractual requirements.

## 4. Evidence quality checklist
- Is the artifact from an authorized, identifiable source?
- Does it cover the right system, people, and time period?
- Is it current and complete rather than a selective screenshot?
- Does it substantiate the actual control requirement?
- Can the assessor reproduce or corroborate the result?
- Is access restricted and sensitivity properly classified?

## 5. Status definitions
**Implementation:** Not assessed / Planned / Partially implemented / Implemented / Not applicable (with rationale).

**Collection:** Not requested / Requested / Received / Validated / Rejected.

**Assessment:** Not reviewed / Sufficient / Insufficient / Needs follow-up.

Do not equate an uploaded artifact with an effective control or formal CMMC assessment.

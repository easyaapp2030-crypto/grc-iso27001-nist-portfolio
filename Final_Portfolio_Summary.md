# Ten-Day GRC Portfolio — Final Summary

## Scenario
Sentinel Defense Solutions (SDS): fictional 100-person DoD contractor processing simulated CUI. The exercise models governance, risk, and compliance workflows; it does not contain real CUI or validated controls.

## Portfolio lifecycle
| Day | Topic | Core work product |
|---|---|---|
| 01 | Organization & GRC foundation | Context, scope, boundary, RACI |
| 02 | Asset inventory & CUI | Assets and data handling context |
| 03 | Risk assessment | 5×5 methodology and risk register |
| 04 | Control selection & mapping | Cross-framework candidate control matrix |
| 05 | Policies & procedures | Information security, access, incident response |
| 06 | Control implementation & evidence | Implementation and evidence tracking |
| 07 | Control assessment & gaps | 12 simulated tests, 10 findings |
| 08 | POA&M & remediation | 10 proposed corrective actions |
| 09 | Continuous monitoring | Eight metrics and reporting model |
| 10 | Executive reporting | Risk brief, decision log, final portfolio |

## Skills demonstrated
RMF-style traceability, CUI scoping, risk assessment, control mapping, governance writing, evidence planning, assessment design, POA&M ownership, metric definition, and executive risk communication.

## Next steps for a production-grade exercise
Reconcile risk IDs and control references across days; select authoritative contract-specific framework revisions; obtain an authorized system boundary and real evidence; validate assessment procedures; establish approved risk ownership and dates; independently retest; and seek authorized management decisions. No live data is needed for this public portfolio.

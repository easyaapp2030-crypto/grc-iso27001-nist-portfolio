# Day 8 — POA&M & Remediation Planning

**Scenario:** Sentinel Defense Solutions (SDS), fictional DoD contractor.

**Objective:** Convert Day 7 simulated gaps into a traceable, owner-driven remediation program.

## Files
- `POAM_Tracker.csv` — 10 corrective-action records linked to Day 7 finding IDs.
- `Remediation_Prioritization.csv` — illustrative priority tiers and dependencies.
- `Remediation_Governance.md` — roles, evidence requirements, escalation, retesting, and closure criteria.

## Practical exercise
Filter the POA&M by high severity, select one action, define measurable milestones and approval-dependent target dates, then identify what evidence an independent assessor would need to close it.

**Important:** All findings, statuses, owners, and priorities are fictional. No corrective action has been performed or verified.

# Day 2 — Asset Inventory & CUI Data Classification

## GRC Portfolio Series | Sentinel Defense Solutions (SDS)

**Project:** Building a GRC Program for a DoD Contractor  
**Organization:** Sentinel Defense Solutions (fictional)  
**Day:** 2 of 10  
**Focus:** Asset Management, Data Classification, and CUI Protection

## 1. Objective

The objective of Day 2 is to identify and document the information assets, systems, applications, and services supporting the fictional SDS environment.

This activity establishes the foundation for risk assessments, security control implementation, and compliance planning.

## 2. Organization Background

Sentinel Defense Solutions (SDS) is a fictional 100-employee defense contractor supporting U.S. Department of Defense programs.

For this case study, SDS processes simulated Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI) within a defined information system environment.

The organization uses managed endpoints, identity and access management, cloud collaboration services, centralized security monitoring, and protected backups.

## 4. Framework Alignment

**NIST SP 800-171:** Supports identification of the systems and information requiring CUI protection.

**CMMC Level 2:** Provides the contractual assessment context for applicable CUI security requirements.

**NIST SP 800-53:** Provides selected control references for asset management, access control, configuration management, and information protection.

**ISO/IEC 27001:** Supports information security asset management, information classification, and assigned responsibilities.

**NIST CSF 2.0:** Supports identifying and managing organizational assets and cybersecurity risks.

These frameworks have different purposes and are not represented as interchangeable compliance requirements.

## 5. Key GRC Considerations

- Identify assets that store, process, or transmit simulated CUI.
- Assign business and technical ownership.
- Document information classification and handling expectations.
- Identify external services, dependencies, and data exchanges.
- Confirm that the inventory aligns with the system boundary established on Day 1.
- Establish a documented basis for the Day 3 risk assessment.

## 6. Expected Outcome

By completing Day 2, SDS will have a documented starting inventory of in-scope information assets and a conceptual understanding of CUI handling and data movement.

These deliverables support subsequent risk identification, control mapping, and compliance gap analysis.

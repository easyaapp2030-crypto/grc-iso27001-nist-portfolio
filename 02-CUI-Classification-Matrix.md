## Handling checks
- Confirm contractual CUI requirements, categories, and authorized systems before processing.
- Inventory storage locations, data owners, authorized users, and approved transfer methods.
- Use least privilege, MFA, logging, encryption, and documented retention/disposal decisions as appropriate.
- Do not send CUI to unapproved public sites or vendor services.
- Escalate uncertainty about classification or markings to the program security/compliance owner.

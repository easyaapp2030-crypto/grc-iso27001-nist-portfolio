# Day 02 — Asset Inventory and CUI Data Classification

**GRC Portfolio Series | Sentinel Defense Solutions (SDS)**

SDS is a **fictional** 100-person DoD contractor. All systems, asset identifiers, ownership assignments, and CUI examples in this folder are simulated for educational purposes. Nothing here is evidence of actual compliance, authorization, or certification.

## Objective
Identify information assets and systems within the proposed CUI boundary, assign owners, document hypothetical data classification and handling, and map the major CUI flows and dependencies.

## Deliverables
1. `01-Asset-Inventory.csv` — sample inventory of in-scope assets, ownership, and data sensitivity.
2. `02-CUI-Classification-Matrix.md` — illustrative classification and handling decisions.
3. `03-CUI-Data-Flow.md` — conceptual Mermaid data-flow diagram.
4. `04-Asset-Ownership-and-Dependencies.md` — accountability and critical dependencies.

## Scope note
CUI markings and handling rules depend on the applicable contract, CUI category, agency guidance, and authorized environment. The examples below do not assert that a particular document is CUI by default. NIST SP 800-171 and applicable CMMC Level 2 requirements are used as design considerations, not proof of assessment.

## Next step
Use this inventory to inform Day 3's risk assessment and risk register.

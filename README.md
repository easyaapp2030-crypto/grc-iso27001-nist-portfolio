# Day 4 — Security Control Selection & Mapping

**10-Day GRC Portfolio | Sentinel Defense Solutions (SDS)**

## Scenario
SDS is a fictional 100-person DoD contractor handling **simulated** Controlled Unclassified Information (CUI). This exercise demonstrates risk-based control selection and cross-framework traceability; it is not a claim of actual CMMC certification or compliance.

## Files
- `day4_control_mapping_matrix.csv` — 12 illustrative risk-to-control mappings.
- `control_selection_methodology.md` — selection criteria, assessment workflow and important limitations.
- `implementation_tracker.csv` — control ownership and evidence planning template.

## How to use
1. Reconcile `R-01` through `R-12` and descriptions against the **actual Day 3 risk register**; identifiers/scenarios here are examples until reconciled.
2. Validate scope and applicable requirements for the CUI system boundary.
3. Verify each cited control against authoritative standards and organizational policy. Crosswalks are **illustrative, not equivalence claims**.
4. Assign implementation status and evidence based on observed facts, not assumptions.
5. Review gaps, record remediation owners/dates, and seek authorization decisions through the appropriate governance process.

## Framework/version note
This exercise uses NIST SP 800-171 **Rev. 2** practice identifiers to illustrate **CMMC Level 2** mapping, plus NIST SP 800-53 Rev. 5 and ISO/IEC 27001:2022 Annex A. NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 3 also exists and has different numbering; confirm contractual and program-specific applicability before using the mappings operationally.

## Portfolio disclaimer
All systems, risks, controls, owners, and evidence examples are fictional. No sensitive or actual CUI is included.

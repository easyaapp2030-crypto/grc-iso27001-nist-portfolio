## Priority gaps
1. **Excessive administrative access (AC-02)** — review privileged roles, enforce least privilege, and document approvals.
2. **Incident response exercise gap (IR-01)** — conduct a tabletop and capture lessons learned.
3. **Stale contractor access (AC-01)** — implement timely offboarding and periodic recertification.
4. **MFA exception (IA-01)** — eliminate legacy bypass or obtain time-bound risk acceptance.
5. **Overdue critical vulnerabilities (RA-01)** — define escalation and remediation SLAs.

## Leadership interpretation
Evidence gaps do not always prove a control is absent; they do mean the assessment cannot substantiate the claimed implementation. Assign owners, deadlines, and closure criteria, then retest.

## Limitations
Not a certification, CMMC assessment, attestation, or security authorization. All findings are fictional.

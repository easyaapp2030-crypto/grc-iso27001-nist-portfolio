# Security Control Assessment Methodology

**Scenario:** Sentinel Defense Solutions (SDS), a fictional DoD contractor handling CUI.

## Purpose and scope
Perform a limited educational assessment of 12 illustrative security controls and associated evidence. This is not a comprehensive assessment of all NIST SP 800-171 requirements, CMMC practices, or NIST SP 800-53 controls.

## Assessment workflow
1. Define system boundary, in-scope assets, and CUI flows.
2. Confirm control applicability and control owners.
3. Request policies, procedures, configurations, logs, tickets, and interview records.
4. Examine artifacts, interview responsible staff, and test configurations where authorized.
5. Record the procedure, sample size, evidence reference, result, gap, and reviewer.
6. Obtain management response, remediation owner, and target date.
7. Retest after remediation and record closure evidence.

## Result scale
- **Satisfied (simulated):** sufficient example evidence meets the defined test criteria.
- **Partially satisfied (simulated):** some criteria met, but an evidence or implementation gap remains.
- **Not satisfied (simulated):** required safeguard or sufficient evidence absent.
- **Not assessed:** test not performed; do not infer effectiveness.

## Evidence integrity
Use unique evidence IDs; record source, owner, capture date, period covered, access restrictions, and integrity checks where appropriate. Never upload live CUI, credentials, secrets, or sensitive configuration details to a public repository.

## Rating and prioritization
Prioritize based on likelihood, impact on CUI/mission, exposure, compensating controls, and dependency. Example severity is illustrative, not a validated organizational risk score.

## Framework caveat
Control identifiers below use illustrative NIST SP 800-171 Rev. 2 references for CMMC Level 2 learning alignment. Validate mappings before operational use.

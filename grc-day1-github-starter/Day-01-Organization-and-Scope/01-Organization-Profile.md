# Organization Profile — Sentinel Defense Solutions (SDS)

**Classification:** Fictional case-study organization; all data simulated.

| Attribute | Scenario |
|---|---|
| Company | Sentinel Defense Solutions (SDS) |
| Industry | U.S. defense contractor, engineering and technical support |
| Employees | 100 (illustrative) |
| Operating model | One primary office plus authorized remote staff in the United States |
| Customers | Fictional DoD program customers and prime contractors |
| Sensitive information | Simulated CUI and ordinary corporate information; no classified data |
| Technology | Contractor-managed endpoints, identity services, a segregated CUI collaboration enclave, approved cloud services, and corporate IT |
| Business-critical processes | Contract delivery, engineering document collaboration, supplier coordination, billing, security incident response |
| Program sponsor | Fictional Chief Operating Officer |
| Security lead | Fictional Information Systems Security Manager (ISSM) |

## Business context

SDS receives simulated CUI-marked engineering documentation under fictional contract requirements. Authorized staff must access and collaborate on these materials without transferring them into general corporate systems or unmanaged devices. Business leadership wants a repeatable program for risk ownership, control implementation, monitoring, and evidence management.

## Security objectives

- Limit CUI access to authorized users and approved devices.
- Maintain accountability for handling, sharing, and retaining CUI.
- Define scope before selecting and assessing safeguards.
- Establish ownership of security decisions and documented risks.
- Protect availability and integrity of contract deliverables.

## Stakeholders

Executive sponsor; ISSM/security lead; IT/cloud operations; engineering/program management; contracts/legal; HR; facilities; external service providers; authorized end users.

**Not modeled:** Classified networks, weapon systems, operational technology, actual DoD system connections, or real federal agency ATO processes.
